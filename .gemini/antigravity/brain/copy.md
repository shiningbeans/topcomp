## Tone Guidelines
- Use "you" and "your" — speak directly to the user
- Prices always formatted: $1,299.99 (with commas and cents)
- Discount percentages: "17% off" (rounded, no decimals)
- Short sentences. No marketing fluff.
- Brand names exactly as the manufacturer uses them (MacBook Pro, ThinkPad)
- Specs in technical format: "16 GB DDR5", "512 GB NVMe SSD", "RTX 4070"
